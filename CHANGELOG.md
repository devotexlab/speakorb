# Changelog / 更新记录

## 0.1.0 — preview / 内测版（unreleased / 未发布）

First public preview. 第一个公开内测版。

- Floating bar with the voice orb: follows your voice volume, shows a timer, turns into a cursor when the text is in.
  带声球的悬浮条：跟音量呼吸、显示计时，写好后变成光标。
- English and Chinese interface, English by default; pick on the first screen or in Settings → General.
  中文和英文界面，默认英文；第一屏或「设置 → 通用」里选。
- English and Mandarin Chinese dictation; new users start with the same language as the interface, changeable in Settings → General and on the "Try it" step.
  中文和英文听写；第一次用时跟界面语言一致，可在「设置 → 通用」和“说一句试试”那一步改。
- Daylight and Night themes, or match the system. 日光、夜光两套主题，也可跟随系统。
- Three-step setup with automatic permission checks and a practice box.
  三步引导：自动检测系统开关，窗口里带练习框。
- History (search, copy, export, delete), custom words (with notes and import), help with a one-click check.
  说过的话（搜索、复制、导出、删除）、常用词（备注、导入）、帮助页一键检查。
- Bring your own service: Gemini, OpenAI, OpenRouter, DeepSeek, Bailian, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint.
  接入自己的服务：Gemini、OpenAI、OpenRouter、DeepSeek、百炼或任何 OpenAI 兼容服务。
- Renamed from the internal codename Talktype; existing history, settings and keys are carried over once.
  由内部代号 Talktype 改名；已有的记录、设置和钥匙会自动搬过来一次。
